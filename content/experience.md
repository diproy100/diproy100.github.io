More than a decade in EPM, from on-premise Hyperion to cloud and OneStream.

### Walmart Global Tech India: Technical Lead
*2020 – Present*

Led the move of large-scale annual operating planning from Essbase to OneStream. I designed business rules and automation that cut manual consolidation effort, built cloud data pipelines on GCP, and prototyped ML-based baseline forecasting.

### Deloitte US-India: Consultant
*2018 – 2020*

Delivered Hyperion-to-PBCS cloud migrations and EPBCS Financials implementations for global clients. I also built automation that saved weeks of manual effort and designed executive P&L dashboards and KPIs.

### Cognizant Technology Solutions: Associate – Projects
*2017 – 2018*

Supported and tuned on-premise Hyperion environments for hundreds of finance users. Essbase performance tuning made queries noticeably faster during month-end close.

### Tata Consultancy Services: IT Analyst
*2013 – 2017*

Designed Hyperion Planning applications from scratch for a major airline group, and built FDMEE integrations to automate data loads from ERP systems.
