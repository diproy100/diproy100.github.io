Hi, I'm **Dipayan Roy**, an Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) architect based in Bengaluru, India.

I have spent more than a decade designing and building the financial planning, budgeting, forecasting and consolidation systems that finance teams rely on, at global enterprises in retail, consumer goods, aviation, agriculture and financial services.

## What I do

- **Architect EPM solutions** on OneStream and Oracle Hyperion (Planning, Essbase, PBCS/EPBCS), from design to go-live.
- **Lead migrations** from legacy on-premise Hyperion and Essbase to modern cloud platforms such as OneStream and Oracle EPM Cloud.
- **Write the engine room code**: business rules, extensibility rules and automation in VB.NET and C#.
- **Build data pipelines** that move large volumes of financial and operational data into EPM, using Python, SQL and Google Cloud Platform.
- **Bring AI/ML into planning**, for example time-series models that produce baseline forecasts automatically.

## Why this blog?

I enjoy the space where finance and engineering meet. Here I write about things I find interesting: EPM design patterns, OneStream and Hyperion tips, Fintech, and software engineering in general.
